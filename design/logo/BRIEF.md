# Wand — logo brief

**Name** Wand (exact spelling, one word, capital W).

**What it is** A native Windows launcher for the games already installed on a
machine. It finds them on disk, shows each with its real cover art and store
description, and starts it — attaching a trainer when mods are switched on.

**Audience** One person: a PC gamer who already owns their games and wants a
quiet, fast front end instead of scrolling a store.

**Adjectives** Cinematic · calm · precise · native · quiet · one-at-a-time.

**Promise** The games you own, in one calm row.

**Where it lives** A 1280×840 window, an `.exe` icon at 16–256 px, and a
Windows taskbar. It must read as a W at 16 px and as a shape at 256 px.

**Constraints** One colour minimum. Works on the app's near-black `#05070f` and
on white. No gradients in the mark itself — the app already carries the game's
colour, so the logo must not compete with it.

## Category clichés — off limits

Gamepads, controllers, joysticks, D-pads, play triangles, glossy 3D cubes,
neon outlines, pixel-art, "power" glyphs, rocket ships.

The gaming shelf is controllers and play buttons. Steam, Unity, Unreal and
Origin own circles and 3D solids. Staying away from all of that is the whole
brief.

## Word map

| Input | Nouns | Metaphors | Opposites |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wand | rod, baton, stick, glint, spark | a conductor's baton; the tap that starts it | a shop, a shelf, a feed |
| Wave | stroke, swish, motion, track | the carousel that slides | a static grid |
| Launch | ramp, rail, edge | the moment before a game opens | browsing forever |
| Mods on | switch, knob, notch | one thing lit while the rest is dim | every tile shouting |

The intersection that belongs to this app alone: **a row of things where one is
lit** — the carousel, the selected tile, the trainer switched on. That is the
one idea worth drawing.

## Mark type choice

- **A — letterform.** The W, with its middle vertex lifted above the cap line,
  so the letter also reads as one tile raised out of a row.
- **B — line.** The same idea as one continuous stroke: a track that dips and
  lifts, ended flat. Fast, no containment.
- **C — pictorial/abstract.** A wand at an angle with a glint at its tip, the
  shaft doubling as the W's first stroke. Says "wand" without drawing magic.

## Assumptions

- The identity is for the app only, not a company with sub-brands.
- A geometric sans will set the wordmark; the letterforms in the mark are drawn
  as paths, not set in a font.
- The mark must survive a one-colour print and a taskbar at 16 px.
